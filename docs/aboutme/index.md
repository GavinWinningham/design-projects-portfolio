My name is Gavin Blake Winningham. I am a junior in Mechanical Engineering at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I was drawn to engineering from working on my car in my free time. many of my closest friends were very much interested in the mechanical components of their vehicles and manipulated them in such ways to fit their image and work in the most efficient manner. This mindset and intrest overtime transfered to me, finding myself researching and trying to further understand not just my vehicle but also the things around me. I started taking computers apart, learned how to make my own, started to learn how to make robots, code, and eventually developed the mindset and the intrest to dive deeper. 

I've since taken that mindset to my academics, leading to my different roles within the school due to my understanding of the materials, becoming a test proctor for the chemistry department to now a TA for the engineering department, assisting with freshman learning.
